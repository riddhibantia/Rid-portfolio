About Riddhi Bantia

A portfolio built on curiosity, craft, and clean execution.

Riddhi Bantia is a design and technology studio where clean interfaces meet intelligent systems. We believe great digital work sits at the intersection of aesthetic precision and technical depth.

Founded as a personal practice, Riddhi Bantia has grown to deliver web experiences, interactive installations, and data-driven tools. Every project starts with understanding the problem — then choosing the right technology to solve it.

Philosophy
  • Design with purpose — every visual choice should serve the content.
  • Code with clarity — clean, maintainable, and performant by default.
  • Ship with confidence — responsive, accessible, and tested.
Approach
  • Discovery — understand the brief, the audience, and the constraints.
  • Design — prototype fast, iterate on real content.
  • Develop — build with modern tools, write code that scales.
  • Deliver — test, optimize, and hand off with documentation.
